# Seed photography

Placeholder product photography for `manage.py seed_demo`. **Not the brand's
imagery** — it exists so that local development has real photographs with real
tonal range, rather than flat colour blocks, while the storefront's layout and
crops are being built.

Every file is 1200 × 1500 (4:5), which is the aspect ratio the storefront's
product tiles and gallery use.
